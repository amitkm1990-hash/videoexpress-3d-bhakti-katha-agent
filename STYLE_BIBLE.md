# Style bible

This repo inherits the format of `videoexpress-3d-bhakti-katha-agent` (Pixar-style 3D Hindi bhakti-katha: golden light, creamy blurred backgrounds, a warm unseen narrator, short lip-synced dialogue, soft background music). Nothing from any other channel's characters, lines, logo or music is used; the story, cast and lines here are original. No new video was studied for this repo; the format notes come from the earlier repo's own study.

## Look

- Pixar-style 3D animation: smooth sculpted faces, soft global illumination, saturated colour, golden light, shallow depth of field with a creamy blurred background.
- Tunnu is a chibi-proportioned child (head about 40% of height, huge glossy eyes). The Guru is a normal-proportioned elder. **The gods follow the real traditional images** (see DEITY_ICONOGRAPHY.md): adult proportions, serene faces, correct arms and attributes.
- Worlds: a Ganga-side ashram courtyard with a Tulsi platform (dusk, night, dawn and festive variants), a pink-gold sunrise sky for the flight, and the milk-white Kshir Sagar with lotuses.
- Camera: medium and medium close-up on the speaker, wide shots for arrivals and the flight, calm push-ins, over-the-shoulder shots for the reveal.

## Sound

- One unseen warm male narrator for the whole video, added as voice-over in the editor.
- Distinct voices: Tunnu bright child, Guru calm raspy elder, Lakshmi soft warm female, Vishnu deep calm resonant, Shesh very deep and slow, Garud strong and proud.
- Speech at a normal conversational pace. Soft devotional instrumental music (flute or strings) under the whole video; light chime at each divine arrival.

## Mouths and ownership

Only the owner of a line speaks it; every other character's mouth is closed for the whole clip. In narrator clips every character's mouth is closed.

## Text on screen

None. No subtitles, captions or watermarks are added or drawn. An optional small channel emblem only if the user supplies one.

## Known limits

- Clips can come out with burned-in garbled captions; prompts never mention text and every clip is checked.
- Only two Reference Photo slots exist; use the speaker + main listener, or a saved group image.
- The model can add extra characters, copy a character, turn a speaker's back to camera, or move a listener's lips; every clip is checked for all of these.
- Four-armed Vishnu and seven-hooded Shesh can glitch; the arm count and hood count are checked in every clip they appear in.
