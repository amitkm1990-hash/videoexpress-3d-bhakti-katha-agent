# अढ़ैया की चतुर्थी — original script, story line and narration

**Working title (video):** जब अढ़ैया की रसोई में गणपति से लेकर महादेव तक आ गए! 😱 | अद्भुत Bhakti Katha | 3D Animation
**Short title (project name in VideoExpress):** Adhaiya Ki Chaturthi
**Format:** 16:9, Hindi, Pixar-style 3D, Image Type 3D
**Clips:** 96 (71 dialogue, 25 narrator) + a 5-clip hook that re-uses four finished clips (clips 60, 57, 77, 80) and one new narrator clip (H5)

This story is original. It follows the *type* of the reference video (a hungry, innocent child devotee; a guru who does not believe; a fasting rule; a repeating visit in which the guest list and the food grow; the child scolds God out of love; the divine family answers gently; the guru finally sees God) but the characters, setting, numbers, jokes and lines are new. Do not paste in or adapt lines from the reference video.

## Story line (one paragraph per act)

**Act 1: Narmada ghat and the Ganesh mandir (clips 01-27).** Hungry orphan Adhaiya, so named because he eats an *adhaiya* (two and a half ser) of puri-halwa, sits on the Narmada ghat. Pandit Keshavanand, the priest of the Ganesh mandir, takes him in. The mandir has one rule: on Sankashti Chaturthi nobody eats grain. Adhaiya refuses to go hungry, so the Pandit lets him cook in the forest, as long as he first offers bhog to Ganpati Bappa by calling him with folded hands.

**Act 2: four Chaturthis, the guest list and the food grow (clips 28-81).**
1. *2.5 ser, Ganesh.* Ganesh ji arrives and eats everything. Adhaiya scolds him lovingly ("ठाकुर, मेरे लिए तो एक पूड़ी भी नहीं छोड़ी!"). The Pandit does not believe him.
2. *5 ser, + Mushakraj.* Adhaiya starts a charcoal tally on a stone. Ganesh arrives with his mouse. The Pandit says it is a dream of hunger.
3. *7.5 ser, + Kartikeya and his peacock.* "आज तो बारात आ गई!" Adhaiya tries to save his share and fails.
4. *10 ser, + Parvati.* Adhaiya proposes a split: "एक पूड़ी तुम्हारी, एक मेरी!" For the first time he is full. Parvati blesses his thali with Annapurna's grace so it never empties. Then a damaru sounds and Mahadev arrives with Nandi and the ganas.

**Act 3: the guru sees (clips 82-96).** Adhaiya runs for the Pandit, who has heard "the same story" four times. He finally trusts the boy, walks to the forest and sees the whole divine family. He weeps: fifty years of puja and no darshan, until a boy's plain love called them. Mahadev gives the moral, and the closing gag stays on Adhaiya: "अगली बार आटे की पूरी बोरी चाहिए!"

## Narration and audio plan

- Narrator clips are voice-over: a warm deep storyteller, calm cadence, slightly slower than the dialogue (about 10 characters per second). Characters on screen stay silent with closed mouths and do a small action; the picture shows what the narrator says.
- Dialogue clips: one on-screen speaker, mouth moving with the line, at a normal conversational pace; listeners stay in the frame with closed mouths. Voices: Adhaiya a bright childlike voice, Pandit a calm older male voice, Ganesh and Kartikeya gentle young male voices, Parvati a warm female voice, Mahadev a deep soft voice.
- Sound beats from the reference style: soft flute and strings under everything, a light laugh after a joke line, a bell or chime when a deity arrives, a damaru roll for Mahadev, birds and river water in S1.
- Subtitles: Hindi, two short lines, white bold Devanagari with a dark outline, bottom centre, added in the editor.
